01 /ABOUT ME

From emissions to mechanisms.
From understanding to action.

I study how emission sources and atmospheric chemical environments shape secondary aerosol formation—and how this understanding can inform air pollution control.

My work combines high-resolution observations, chemical thermodynamics and kinetics, source apportionment, atmospheric modeling, and interpretable machine learning. I received my Ph.D. in Environmental Science from Nankai University in 2025 and was a CSC-supported visiting doctoral researcher at the University of Birmingham in 2024.
